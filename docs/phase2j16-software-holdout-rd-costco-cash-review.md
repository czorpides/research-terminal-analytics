## 2. RESEARCH capitalisation life stress — no invented R&D asset or adjusted EBIT

Source **annual fiscal-year R&D expense** cohorts in original 3-year SEC annual statement columns:

| Fiscal year | Adobe original 10-K | Autodesk original 10-K |
|---|---:|---:|
| Third oldest FY | 2023: 3,473 | 2024: 1,373 |
| Second oldest FY | 2024: 3,944 | 2025: 1,485 |
| Latest completed FY | 2025: 4,294 | 2026: 1,643 |

A wholly hypothetical **end-of-year cohort recognition** convention assumes the latest annual R&D investment is held as a full unamortised asset at latest FY-end; preceding cohort shares remaining are `(L−1)/L`, `(L−2)/L`, etc. This is **NOT GAAP**, management guidance, or accepted Greenblatt EBIT.

```text
Illustrative closing R&D capital stock (life L, whole FY cohort vintage k)
  = sum[ original GAAP annual R&D expense in FY(latest − k) × max(0, L−k)/L ]
  for k = 0 .. L−1. If ANY required FY source is missing -> NULL.

Never append TTM R&D as an additional annual FY cohort: it overlaps an earlier FY!
Never calculate adjusted earnings or software ROC from the closing stock alone:
  a matching in-year amortisation opening/closing history is NOT independently reconstructed.
```

| Hypothetical economic life | Adobe R&D stock, USD m at FY2025 | Autodesk R&D stock, USD m at FY2026 | Approved adjusted EBIT/ROC |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2 years | **6,266.000000** | **2,385.500000** | **No** |
| 3 years | **8,081.000000** | **3,090.666667** | **No** |
| 5 years | **Unavailable:** requires FY2021/22 annual data | **Unavailable:** requires FY2022/23 data | **No** |

Both the definition of a new research investment asset and its financing/cost of capital require policy approval. Recognised acquired technology and goodwill, lease capital, contract acquisition assets and deferred-revenue liabilities all require a consistent analytical decision. These research stock stresses **do not** add any value to the issuer's balance sheet and are not passed to the Phase 2J10 or Radar engine. **No adjusted operating profit, economic ROC, magic ranking or implied price is produced.**
